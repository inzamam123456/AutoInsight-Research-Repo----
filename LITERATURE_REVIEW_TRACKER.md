## Extraction template
For each paper, capture full title, authors, year, venue/publisher, DOI or stable URL, research problem, methodology, dataset/sample size, relevant features, evaluation metrics and reported results, advantages, limitations, and relevance to AutoInsight.

## Research integrity
Use original publications for bibliographic fields and reported results. Distinguish paper findings from the team's interpretation. Do not infer missing metrics or claim a paper validates AutoInsight itself.
